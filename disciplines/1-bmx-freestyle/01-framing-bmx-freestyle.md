# Framing Sheet — BMX Freestyle (Park)
**Week 35 · Aug 17-23, 2026 · LA2028**

## 1. What "performance" means in this discipline
Olympic BMX Freestyle is contested only in **Park** format (Flatland is not an Olympic event). Each athlete performs **two 60-second runs** on a course of ramps, rails, and features. A panel of 5 judges gives an overall score on a 0-100 scale (sometimes displayed as 0.00-99.99), based on an **overall impression** combining several criteria: difficulty, execution (clean technical delivery of a trick), creativity, use of the course, amplitude, variety of tricks, flow, and clean landings.

- **Qualification round**: average of the two runs.
- **Final**: only the best of the two runs counts.

→ Performance is therefore **a subjective judges' score, not a timed result**, which has a direct methodological implication: inter-judge variance and score comparability across events will be a point of caution in the statistical analysis.

## 2. LA2028 qualification calendar
- **Total quota**: 24 athletes (12 men / 12 women), maximum 2 men + 2 women per nation.
- **Official qualification period**: from November 3, 2026 through the last Olympic Qualifier Series event in 2028.
- **Qualification pathways** (mixed system, to confirm precisely in the UCI PDF):
  - UCI world ranking (BMX Freestyle World Cup, 2026-2028 seasons)
  - Olympic Qualifier Series (similar to Shanghai/Budapest before Paris 2024)
  - Continental quota places
  - Automatic host nation place (USA)
- **Olympic competition**: between July 14-30, 2028, at Sepulveda Basin Recreation Area (Valley Complex 1), Los Angeles. Exact session dates still to be confirmed in the detailed competition schedule.

## 4. Performance indicators to track (leads for the analysis)
- Qualification average score vs. final score (qualif/final gap = pressure management)
- Best-score progression over 2-3 World Cup seasons
- Consistency (standard deviation between the two runs of a heat)
- Age at first international medal vs. current age
- Number of World Cup / OQS final appearances during the qualification period
- Breakdown by nation (medal density, depth of bench)
